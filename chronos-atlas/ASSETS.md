# Rendering assets and provenance

These assets are visual material references. None is evidence for the historical material, weather, colour or dimensions of a particular Alexandrian building.

- **Seaworn Sandstone Brick**, Dimitrios Savva / Poly Haven. Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps at 2K. https://polyhaven.com/a/seaworn_sandstone_brick — CC0.
- **Beige Wall 001**, photography by Dimitrios Savva, processing by Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven. Plaster diffuse, normal and roughness maps at 2K. https://polyhaven.com/a/beige_wall_001 — CC0.
- **Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)**, original by Greg Zaal, sky edit by Jarod Guest / Poly Haven. HDR lighting map at 1K. https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky — CC0. This is a modern sky-lighting reference, not a historical view of Alexandria.
- **Water normal texture**, Three.js examples, https://threejs.org/examples/textures/waternormals.jpg — Three.js MIT-licensed example asset. Source repository: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js . Copyright Three.js authors; MIT licence retained below.
- Geometry, procedural wood texture, camera logic, scene composition and synthesized ambience: original project code. No AI-generated picture is used as the 3D world.

Poly Haven licence: https://polyhaven.com/license . Original metadata is retained under `public/materials/`.

## Ice Age scene materials

Photographic CC0 maps from Poly Haven, retrieved 8 November 2026 (1K JPG).

- Rocky Terrain 02, Amal Kumar: https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02
- Snow 01, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_01
- Rock Boulder Dry, Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry

Per-asset provenance: public/materials/ice-age/provenance.json. These are visual materials, not historical evidence. Mammoths, terrain, grass, rocks and fur texture are original procedural forms authored for this prototype; no external animal models or AI-generated landscape image are used. The existing CC0 sky and MIT Three.js water normal are reused.
